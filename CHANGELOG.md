# Changelog

## 1.3.0

### Added

- Filter sets by where they come from. Open Filter, choose Sources, and tick the categories you want to see. The Sets tab offers raid, PvP, covenant, heritage, cosmetic, dungeon, trading post, and misc, and the Extra tab offers classic, quest, dungeon, recolor, garrison, island expedition, warfronts, holiday, trading post, misc, and trash.
- A Cosmetic category for the outfit collections any class can wear, so they can be hidden separately from class armour.
- Source filtering now works on the Sets tab as well as the Extra tab. Previously only the Extra tab had it.

### Fixed

- Check All and Uncheck All no longer close the filter menu, so you can clear a group and carry on adjusting the rest.
- Changing a filter no longer leaves the preview showing a set you have just filtered out. The list and the model on the right could end up showing two different sets, and the preview now moves to the top of the new list instead.
- Recolor sets no longer go missing from the Extra tab. Around 80 of them were hidden no matter how your filters were set.
- Sets sold through the in-game shop are now recognised as shop sets, so they pick up the shop icon and follow the Trading Post filter.
